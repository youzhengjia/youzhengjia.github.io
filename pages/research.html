The Local Air Pollution of Data Centers

Abstract: This paper estimates the causal effect of data centers on local sulfur dioxide (SO2) air pollution in the United States. Data centers test backup diesel generators after receiving air permits, and that commissioning process emits SO2. I combine facility-level permit data with satellite-based grid-level measurements and estimate the effect of data centers permitted between 2021 and 2022 using a stacked spatial difference-in-differences design. In the year when data centers get permits, SO2 rises by 0.16 ppb within 10 km, or 0.13 standard deviations. The SO2-related mortality costs within the treatment region are around $800,000 in 2006 dollars in one year.
